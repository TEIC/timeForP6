It’s Time for P6?!

by the TEI Technical Council

  • TEI 2026: Thurs. 13 August @ 1pm, Vancouver, BC

Problems with P5

  • P5 class system is confusing.
  • P5 has too much prescriptiveness.
  • P5 has too many ways of doing the same thing.
    • Yes, we realize the previous too points are contradictory.
    • Is P5 confusing for beginners? or really, everybody?
  • In P5, some metadata and non-metadata elements are muddled and may need clearer distinction.
  • Maintenance of P5 is complicated
    • Maintaining the Stylesheets is very complex.
  • We need context-dependent content models, if not full-fledged co-occurence constraints.
  • The integration of existing standards is complicated.
  • The problems with P5 are entrenched over a long tim e, and it's too difficult to introduce new features.
